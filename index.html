What I found when I measured it

  1. 01

    AI-made ads can move people.

    The AI-made ad shifted viewers' mood as much as the human-made one did.

  2. 02

    Emotion is not recall.

    In both versions, some viewers enjoyed the story and forgot the brand. The brand has to be part of the story.

  3. 03

    Showing beats telling.

    Viewers who doubted AI could move them changed their view after watching. Be open about how AI was used.

Research

One ad, made twice, and measured.

  1. Do people feel an AI-made ad the way they feel one made by people? I made both versions of the same one-minute ad and tested them.

    • Created a fictional career-services brand and wrote the ad's story, voiceover and slogans.
    • Generated one version from 12 scene prompts in Hailuo AI. Directed and filmed student theatre actors for the other, shot for shot, and edited both the same way.
    • Ran 20 lab sessions: EEG headset, eye tracker, a mood scale before and after, and a 10-minute interview with each viewer.
    • Coded the interviews with the Gioia method and matched the themes with the EEG and gaze data.
    Tools
    EMOTIV EPOC+, Gazepoint GP3 HD, PANAS-SF, Hailuo AI, Ideogram, OpenAI text-to-speech, VN Video Editor, Audacity

  2. Shitova, M., Kabalska, A., Varga, Á., & Wagner, R. (2026). Minds Versus Codes: Triangulating Neurophysiological and Qualitative Responses to AI-Generated Versus Human-Made Video Advertisements. Psychology & Marketing.

    • The journal version of the thesis experiment, written with co-authors from universities in Germany, Poland and Hungary.
    • The AI-made ad moved viewers' mood as much as the human-made one. Viewers who doubted AI could move them changed their view after watching.
    • In both versions, emotion alone did not make people remember the brand. The brand had to be part of the story.
